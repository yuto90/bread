Describe connections only

Begin with bread 0.1, declare a part with part led: led-5mm-red, then connect its pins with led.K -- uno.GND. Use // for comments.

A deliberately small playground

Try one or three to six resistor/LED branches, the mixed-parts study, or the diode/LED family with at most one optional 100nF bypass capacitor. 1N4148 uses A/K (black band K); C315C104 uses non-polar 1/2 terminals. Other combinations and manual layout are not supported. Diagrams can have crossing wires; a crossing is not a junction.
